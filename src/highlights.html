Club Spotlight

Featured Clubs of the Semester

These clubs stood out this semester through exceptional activities, impact, and member engagement.

🤖

Robotics Club

Club of the Semester

The Robotics Club won 1st place at the National Robotics Championship 2025 with their autonomous drone delivery system "AeroSend". The team of 6 students spent 4 months designing, building, and testing their robot.

Members: 42  |  Founded: 2018

🏆 National Champions Innovation
🌿

Eco & Environment Club

Most Impactful Club

The Eco Club's "Zero Waste Campus" campaign reduced single-use plastic on campus by 65% this semester. They also partnered with the city government to plant 200 trees in local parks.

Members: 78  |  Founded: 2015

🌍 Sustainability Community Impact
🎭

Drama & Theatre Society

Best Cultural Club

Their original production "Echoes of Tomorrow" sold out 3 consecutive shows and was invited to perform at the National Youth Theatre Festival. The script won a national creative writing award.

Members: 35  |  Founded: 2012

🎭 Sold Out Shows National Award
