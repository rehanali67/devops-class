Simple Process

How to Join a Club

Getting started is easy. Follow these four steps to become a club member.

1

Browse the Clubs

Visit our Clubs page to explore all 20+ student clubs across five categories. Find the one that matches your interests and passion.

2

Fill the Application Form

Complete the membership form below with your details and preferred club. Most clubs have open enrollment; some may require a short interview or audition.

3

Wait for Confirmation

The club coordinator will review your application and send a confirmation email within 3–5 business days. Check your university email regularly.

4

Attend Your First Session

Once confirmed, you'll receive a welcome kit with the meeting schedule, club guidelines, and everything you need to dive in. Welcome aboard! 🎉
